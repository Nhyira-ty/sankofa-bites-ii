Recipe for Ghana Jollof Rice

Traditional, smoky West African masterpiece

🍲

Required Ingredients:

Long grain perfumed Jasmine rice (3 cups), Tomato paste (4 tbsp), Fresh onions, Garlic cloves, Fresh ginger root, Fresh blended tomatoes, Rich stock (chicken or beef), Curry aromatic spice powder, Dry thyme, Dried bay leaves, Spicy scotch bonnet pepper, Vegetable cooking oil, Fine salt.

Step-by-Step Strategy:

  1. 1. Fry sliced onions in hot vegetable oil, add tomato paste and cook down until dark red.
  2. 2. Pour in your blended fresh tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and hot pepper mix; fry until oil floats clean to the top surface layer.
  3. 3. Stir seasoned meat stock and dry aromatics into the base stew to create your flavor footprint.
  4. 4. Wash your jasmine rice thoroughly, stir flat into the stew mix, wrap securely with foil to lock steam, and cook over a minimal low heat burner for 25-30 minutes until smoky and soft.
