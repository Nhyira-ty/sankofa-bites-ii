🇬🇭 Authentic Ghana Jollof

The legendary, smoky West African classic

Ingredients

  • Long grain perfumed Jasmine rice (3 cups)
  • Tomato paste (4 tbsp) & blended fresh tomatoes
  • Onions, garlic, ginger, and scotch bonnet peppers (blended)
  • Chicken/Beef stock + Curry powder, Thyme, and Bay leaves

The Strategy

  1. The Stew: Fry sliced onions, add tomato paste, and stir constantly till deep red. Add blended tomato/pepper base and cook down until oil separates.
  2. The Base: Pour in your savory seasoned stock and aromatic spices. Bring to a roaring boil.
  3. The Rice: Wash rice thoroughly until water runs perfectly clear. Stir it evenly into the stew base.
  4. The Steam: Cover tightly with foil/foil wrap and a lid. Turn heat to ultra-low. Let it steam in its own moisture for 25-30 mins until grains are tender and perfectly smoky.
